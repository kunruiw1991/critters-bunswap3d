## ✨ Game Highlights
Inspired by hilarious web-novel treasure-interception & mind-reading tropes (*"隐藏信息读心镜 + 后山工兵铲挖宝 + 坑里留个热腾腾大包子"*):
- **🔍 1:1 3D Crystal X-Ray Magnifying Lens (`隐藏信息读心镜`)**: Drag the glowing cyan crystal magnifying glass over the Golden-Haloed Chosen One Critters (`v11` DogDay & `v11` CatNap) to pop open their Mystery Clouds and reveal which of the 4 Emblem Dirt Mounds (`☀️ Star`, `🌙 Moon`, `💎 Ruby`, `🍀 Clover`) hides their buried treasure!
- **⛏️ 1:1 3D Golden Shovel Scoop (`后山工兵铲`)**: Drag the gleaming 3D Golden Shovel onto the target mound to launch a `1/120s` substepped shower of 3D dirt clods and pop out the **3D Golden Imperial Treasure Chest (`帝兵金宝箱`)**!
- **🥟 Steaming Bamboo Basket Bun Swap (`坑里留个热腾腾大包子`)**: Drag a plump, bouncy **3D Steamed Bun (`大包子`)** from the woven bamboo steamer into the open pit before the Chosen One arrives — watching them cheerily hug the warm Steamed Bun while you pocket the Golden Treasure Chest!
- **🔇 100% Zero-Text & Zero-Voiceover Pure Visual UI**: Built for 5-year-old players with zero words or numbers, custom procedural WebAudio ASMR, and a 5-Critter Top-3 Podium progression system.
